# Code of Conduct

English · [한국어](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ko.md)

## Our pledge

We want MyDB IDE to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Help newcomers find their way.
- Keep discussions on topic and focused on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing discussions

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the contact on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/taeyun1215) or a private security advisory. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban contributors depending on the severity.
